I am a PhD candidate in Mechanical Engineering at the [University of Illinois, Urbana-Champaign (UIUC)](https://illinois.edu), advised by Prof. [Leonardo P. Chamorro](https://mechse.illinois.edu/people/profile/lpchamo). Prior coming to UIUC, I was an Undergraduate Special Student in Course 2 (MechE) at [Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)](https://web.mit.edu) during my senior year. I obtained my B.S. in Theoretical & Applied Mechanics at the [Southern University of Science & Technology (SUSTech)](https://www.sustech.edu.cn/en/) in Shenzhen, China.

My research interests span multiple topics, including:
- Turbulence/Aerodynamics
- Reduced-Order Modeling (ROM)
- Neural Operator Learning
- Multiphysics Foundation Models
- Light-Field Imaging & Sensing

Broadly speaking, I am interested in Applied Math & Modeling on real-world complex systems, including but not limited to physical systems like fluid flows. Feel free to reach out if you are interested in research collaboration or discussion.
